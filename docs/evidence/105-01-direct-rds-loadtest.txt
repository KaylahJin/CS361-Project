Reading
-------
- 38 concurrent Lambda containers opened 38 DB connections (one per container
  for the single query), ~34% of the ~112 ceiling. Connections lingered a few
  minutes after the burst (idle containers holding their pg pool) then released.
  Still well under the ceiling, 0 errors.
- Latency (2-5 s) is dominated by Lambda cold-start init and per-container
  first-connect, NOT by the database. Server-side Duration (<=2.5 s, excl.
  init) is well under the client total, and DB connections never contended.
- No RDS Proxy is in the path; the direct connection absorbed 50 concurrent
  requests with 0 errors and ~34% connection-ceiling use.

Conclusion: the direct Lambda->RDS connection supports the expected prototype
load (<=50 concurrent reads) with large headroom and no connection exhaustion.
See docs/direct-rds-connection-assessment.md for the full write-up.
